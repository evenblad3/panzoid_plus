Tweaks change how Clipmaker runs on this machine. They are never saved into your clip, so nobody who opens your project gets them. Reload the Clipmaker page after you change one, and save your project first, because a reload drops anything unsaved. Some of them hold on to parts of Clipmaker that are not meant to be used from outside. If the site changes and this extension has not been updated for a while, a tweak can stop working without saying so.
drop a .pz here, or click to pick one
A .pz file is a gzipped tar. Inside sit the project, which describes the whole scene, a small meta file, and every sound, image, font and model the project uses, each one stored under the sha256 of its own contents rather than under its name. Drop one here and the media comes back out named the way you imported it, with models rebuilt as .obj.

Panzoid+ sat untouched for about three years. I came back to it because what it did was never quite the thing I wanted it to do, and I would rather finish it than leave it sitting in the store half done. Most of what is here now was rewritten from scratch.

If it is useful to you, a review helps more than anything else I can do for it. If something is missing, or behaves stupidly, tell me and I will look at it. Discord @wzrterfx.

There is no Firefox port and I am not planning one. The tweaks reach into Clipmaker's own internals from a main world content script, and keeping that working against one browser is already enough.

The extension is open for contribution. The main branch of development is the Repository. That link may move, so I am not promising it stays valid.

Next on the list are tweaks that fix the bugs in Clipmaker that have been sitting there for seven years or more and were never going to be fixed upstream.
